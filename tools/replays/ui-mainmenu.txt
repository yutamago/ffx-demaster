# ui.menus = ps2, main menu: what mod/MainMenuPatches.cs draws, through tools/calllist (the @ lines
# are its commands; docs/ui.md, "The main menu"). Needs the converter's ui output as a mod (the
# PS2 atlas ps2win, the PS2 fonts ps2fnt). From save slot 1 (salvage ship deck): open the menu,
# shoot the HD draw, load the PS2 atlas and fonts (PhyreScene_loadMenuTexture), point the main
# draw, the option window and the help bar at call lists of the PS2 draw and the background at the
# PS2 gradient, shoot again; the 4:3 write (render.aspect) and a shot.
load direct 1 cdsp0000 lockstep
lockstep on
pin
wait 120
press triangle 4 40
wait 30
shot $OUT\main-1-hd.bmp
@base
# the PS2 text line (mod Ps2Menu.install: no glyph y offset, 20-line line feeds)
@alloc YF 4096
@protect YF 0x008e9000 4096
@ps2font YF
@alloc N 4096
write {N} 2f4646585f446174612f47616d65446174612f505333446174612f6d656e755f75732f44334431312f70733277696e2e6464732e706879726500
call 0x006651d0 {=N}
@alloc F 4096
write {F} 2f4646585f446174612f47616d65446174612f505333446174612f6d656e755f75732f44334431312f707332666e742e6464732e706879726500
call 0x006651d0 {=F}
wait 20
@alloc M 65536
@alloc C 65536
@alloc B 65536
@alloc G 4096
@thunk M
@thunk C
@thunk B
@thunk G
@alloc S 4096
@protect S 0x008f5c00 128
@ps2bg G
@ps2menu M C B N F
# the PS2 menu scenes the mod starts at the screen's first draw (Ps2Menu.scenes); their slide-in
@alloc SC 4096
@scenes SC scene26
wait 40
wait 2
shot $OUT\main-2-ps2.bmp
write 0x00c34154 abaaaa3f
wait 2
shot $OUT\main-3-ps2-43.bmp
