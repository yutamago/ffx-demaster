# The live music engine: field music, a battle and back (docs/music.md,
# "Phase 2"). After the load, waits for demaster_live.dll ("LIVE" at 0x00400070,
# tools/live-test.sh), save 5 (Djose highroad, kami0100: Spira Unplugged),
# the original soundtrack switched on, 10 s of field music, a battle
# (genk16_03) whose monsters (actors 0x14-0x16 of *0x011334d4, 0xf90
# bytes each, HP at +0x5d0) get 1 HP and fall to three attacks, the
# victory fanfare, the results closed with circle, 30 s on the field: the
# field music the battle paused (the game's replay point) comes back.
load direct 5 kami0100 lockstep
lockstep on
waitfor 0x00400070:4 4c495645 ffffffff 100000
wait 10
write 0x0112d07c 01
callnext 0x008cc170
wait 10
audiomark start
wait 300
audiomark battle
battle 33 0 3
waitfor 0x0112a8e0:1 01 ff 4000
writep 0x011334d4 0x13d10 01000000
writep 0x011334d4 0x14ca0 01000000
writep 0x011334d4 0x15c30 01000000
wait 60
press circle 4 30
press circle 4 400
press circle 4 30
press circle 4 400
press circle 4 30
press circle 4 400
audiomark fanfare
wait 200
shot $OUT\music-live-return-1.bmp
press circle 4 60
press circle 4 60
press circle 4 60
press circle 4 60
press circle 4 60
press circle 4 60
audiomark field
wait 900
shot $OUT\music-live-return-2.bmp
audiomark end
