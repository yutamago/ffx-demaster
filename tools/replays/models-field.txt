# Field models: save 1 (salvage ship deck, cdsp0000), Tidus standing, then
# walking. Shots 1 and 2 are 8 frames apart (idle animation), 3 and 4 while
# he walks (adapted from ffx-rust tools/replays/field-walk.txt, whose wait
# pins the shot to the event's clock).
load direct 1 cdsp0000 lockstep
lockstep on
pin
waitfor 0x01325d94:4 40000000 c0ffffff 1500
wait 37
shot $OUT\field-1.bmp
wait 8
shot $OUT\field-2.bmp
pad down
wait 20
shot $OUT\field-3.bmp
wait 6
shot $OUT\field-4.bmp
pad
pad left
wait 12
pad
wait 30
shot $OUT\field-5.bmp
