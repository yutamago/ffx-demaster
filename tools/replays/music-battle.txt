# The battle theme from the "Original" soundtrack bank (docs/music.md, "In
# the game"): save 5 (Djose highroad), the save's soundtrack byte
# (g_SaveData 0x0112ca90 + 0x5ec) set to 1 and iMusicRefreshBgmType
# (0x008cc170) queued, as the game's menu does, then a battle (genk16_03),
# whose music FMOD reads from sound_pc/music_ps2. With FFX_AUDIO_WAV the
# capture runs from `audiomark start` (the battle) to `audiomark end`.
load direct 5 kami0100 lockstep
lockstep on
wait 10
write 0x0112d07c 01
callnext 0x008cc170
wait 10
audiomark start
battle 33 0 3
waitfor 0x0112a8e0:1 01 ff 4000
wait 60
shot $OUT\music-battle-1.bmp
wait 1200
audiomark end
shot $OUT\music-battle-2.bmp
