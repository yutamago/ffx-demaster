# Besaid beach (bsil0000) from save 3 with the "Original" soundtrack: the
# save's soundtrack byte (g_SaveData 0x0112ca90 + 0x5ec) set to 1 and
# iMusicRefreshBgmType (0x008cc170) queued, as the game's menu does, so
# FMOD switches to sound_pc/music_ps2 and restarts the map's music from the
# PS2 bank (docs/music.md, "In the game"). With FFX_AUDIO_WAV the capture
# runs from `audiomark start` (after the switch) to `audiomark end`.
load direct 3 bsil0000 lockstep
lockstep on
pin
waitfor 0x01325d94:4 40000000 c0ffffff 1500
write 0x0112d07c 01
callnext 0x008cc170
wait 30
audiomark start
wait 300
shot $OUT\music-besaid-1.bmp
wait 900
audiomark end
shot $OUT\music-besaid-2.bmp
