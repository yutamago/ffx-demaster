# The live music engine (docs/music.md, "Phase 2") in the harness: after
# the load the
# replay waits until demaster_live.dll is in (`tools/live-test.sh` injects
# it with live-inject.exe; the DLL writes "LIVE" at 0x00400070), then as
# music-battle.txt: save 5 (Djose highroad), the original soundtrack
# switched on (the save's byte 0x0112ca90 + 0x5ec, iMusicRefreshBgmType),
# 20 s of the field's music, a battle (genk16_03) and 30 s of it. With
# FFX_AUDIO_WAV the capture runs from `audiomark start` to `audiomark end`.
load direct 5 kami0100 lockstep
lockstep on
waitfor 0x00400070:4 4c495645 ffffffff 100000
wait 10
write 0x0112d07c 01
callnext 0x008cc170
wait 10
audiomark start
wait 600
audiomark battle
battle 33 0 3
waitfor 0x0112a8e0:1 01 ff 4000
wait 900
audiomark end
shot $OUT\music-live-battle.bmp
