# Battle subtitles (ui.battle_subtitles, docs/patches.md): the harness can't
# patch code, so this sets the bit the mod's patch makes the getter answer
# (g_SaveMenu 0x0113079c bit 9) through the game's own setter,
# MsSetSaveConfigSubTitle (0x00785f80). Developer save 22 (Luca, as ffx-rust's
# devsave-22-luca.txt), then the docks' battle (field 12, lchb02;
# formation lchb02_00, whose script says "Whoa! What are they?" at the
# start). The developer save has the bit set already (g_SaveMenu 0x0001a763),
# so the replay clears it first and sets it again through the setter. Shots
# (battle-subtitles-<n>.bmp) every 40 updates from the battle's start: with
# the bit, "Tidus: Whoa! What are they?" and Lulu's two lines at the bottom
# (shots 1-6, checked 2026-11-02); without it (comment out the call), none.
load devsave 22 lchb0201 lockstep
lockstep on
pin
waitfor 0x01325d94:4 40000000 c0ffffff 1500
wait 150
write 0x0113079c 63a50100
call 0x00785f80 1
read 0x0113079c 4
battle 12 0 0
waitfor 0x0112a8e0:1 01 ff 4000
read 0x0113079c 4
wait 20
shot $OUT\battle-subtitles-1.bmp
wait 40
shot $OUT\battle-subtitles-2.bmp
wait 40
shot $OUT\battle-subtitles-3.bmp
wait 40
shot $OUT\battle-subtitles-4.bmp
wait 40
shot $OUT\battle-subtitles-5.bmp
wait 40
shot $OUT\battle-subtitles-6.bmp
wait 40
shot $OUT\battle-subtitles-7.bmp
wait 40
shot $OUT\battle-subtitles-8.bmp
wait 60
shot $OUT\battle-subtitles-9.bmp
wait 60
shot $OUT\battle-subtitles-10.bmp
