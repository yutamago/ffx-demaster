# The live music engine through a whole battle (docs/music.md, "Phase
# 2"): after the load, waits for demaster_live.dll ("LIVE" at 0x00400070, tools/live-test.sh),
# save 1 (the salvage ship, cdsp0000), the original soundtrack switched on,
# 10 s of the field's music, the first formation (attacked twice, as
# ffx-rust's battle-attack.txt: both enemies fall), the victory fanfare,
# the results closed with circle, and 20 s back on the field.
load direct 1 cdsp0000 lockstep
lockstep on
waitfor 0x00400070:4 4c495645 ffffffff 100000
wait 10
write 0x0112d07c 01
callnext 0x008cc170
wait 10
audiomark start
wait 300
audiomark battle
battle 3 0 0
waitfor 0x0112a8e0:1 01 ff 4000
wait 60
press circle 4 30
press circle 4 400
press circle 4 30
press circle 4 400
audiomark fanfare
wait 300
shot $OUT\music-live-victory-1.bmp
press circle 4 60
press circle 4 60
press circle 4 60
press circle 4 60
press circle 4 60
press circle 4 60
audiomark field
wait 600
shot $OUT\music-live-victory-2.bmp
audiomark end
