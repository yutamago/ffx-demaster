# Overdrive and summon effects, cut down from ffx-rust's
# battle-overdrive.txt (its comments explain the writes and waits): save 5,
# Djose highroad (battle 33 0 3), party Yuna, Tidus, Wakka, full gauges.
# Tidus's Spiral Cut (magic 0x186, magic_0390), Yuna summons Valefor
# (entry 0x253, magic_0595), Valefor's Energy Blast (0x25c, magic_0604).
# Shots (fx-overdrive-<n>.bmp): 1 Spiral Cut, 2 and 3 Valefor's entry,
# 4 and 5 Energy Blast.
load direct 5 kami0100 lockstep
lockstep on
pin
wait 10
write 0x011307e8 01000405ff020306
battle 33 0 3
waitfor 0x0112a8e0:1 01 ff 4000

writep 0x011334d4 0x823c 14

press circle 4 10

press circle 4 30
waitfor 0x0133d734:2 0000 ffff 600
wait 40
