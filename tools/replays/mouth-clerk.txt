# fixes.npc_mouths (docs/patches.md): the agency clerk (n058, save 5, chr record 7) made to talk
# for 30 updates by writing chr +0x4d4; the mouth slot's FRAME (texture animation 7, slot 3 at
# work +0xa8: 1 shows the closed frame, 0 the open one) and the talk and jaw words before, while
# and after. Unpatched PC: 1, 1, 0; the PS2: 1, 0, 1.
load direct 5 kami0100 lockstep
lockstep on
pin
wait 30
wait 2
readp 0x023c44e4 0x3b80 4
readp 0x023c44e4 0x4280 4
readp 0x02310154 0xa8 4
readp 0x023c44e4 0x4054 12
shot $OUT\mouth-clerk-0before.bmp
writep 0x023c44e4 0x4054 01
wait 20
shot $OUT\mouth-clerk-1talk.bmp
readp 0x02310154 0xa8 4
readp 0x023c44e4 0x4054 12
wait 10
writep 0x023c44e4 0x4054 00
wait 40
readp 0x02310154 0xa8 4
readp 0x023c44e4 0x4054 12
shot $OUT\mouth-clerk-2after.bmp
